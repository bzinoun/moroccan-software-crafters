# Communication rules (SoCraTes Maroc / MSCA)

- Partner emails: short, formal French.
- Public posts (LinkedIn): English. Short, punchy bullets; LinkedIn has no native lists, so use symbol bullets with blank lines.
- Organizer group chat (WhatsApp): French-English mix. Keep the mix when you correct a message.
- No em dashes, anywhere.
- When polishing a message: keep the tone and the argument order, make concise corrections, do not rewrite formally.

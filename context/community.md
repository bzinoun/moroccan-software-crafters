## Formats
- Meetup: Saturday morning, about every two months, hosted by a partner company, about 30 to 50 people; topics technical, agile, DevOps, data, cloud, methodology.
- Meetup agenda (unconference style): check-in and coffee, welcome, host keynote, 4-5 lightning talks, marketplace (topic proposals and votes), group discussions, closing, group photo, networking.
- SoCraTes Maroc: annual weekend event, deep technical discussions. 1st edition Marrakech, March 2022, AXA sponsor.
- Attendees at corporate venues bring the carte nationale for check-in.

## Money situation
- Organizers pay the SoCraTes funding gap from their own pockets and do the meetup preparation and facilitation. Most host companies get the marketing, recruitment and brand benefits without sponsoring SoCraTes.
- Since 2024, 5 of 7 meetup hosts never sponsored SoCraTes. SoCraTes 2025 had a 140,000-210,000 MAD sponsor shortfall against a 514,500 MAD budget, mostly hotel costs.
- 2027 goal: grow the association, make meetups pay, learn what partners need.

## 2027 revenue models (27 Oct 2026, proposal for the organizer group)
- Principle: companies pay, crafters never pay for access.
- Model 1, main product, Meetup clé en main: base 10,000 MAD (orga, content, com, catering, recap post, logo, 5 min recruitment slot, job offers on the site, 30 to 40 devs; the company gives the room). Option A +5,000: talent list and insight notes. Option B +5,000: MSCA brings the room. Replaces Host/Host+/Full service.
- Model 2, annual company membership: one guaranteed meetup, logo on the site all year, job offers, priority in the host queue; second step after 3 paid meetups.
- Model 3, sponsor bundle: base meetup included in a SoCraTes sponsor pack, options stay paid. Compromise for ATS and long-term partners.
- Model 4, workshop/kata at the company for its own team, paid per session; legal check needed (close to a training service).
- Model 5, BBL: 45 min lunch talk at the company for a small fee.
- Model 6, free lane: meetup as today for startups, schools and partners that cannot pay.
- Model 7, merch (t-shirt, hoodie, stickers) bought by crafters by choice, print on demand; needs a design first; legal check for sales by an association.
- Out: crafter subscription, paid meetup tickets, selling the attendee list (law 09-08).
- Lead form on the new site (Badr-Eddine's idea, can go live first): company, contact, format, preferred month, room available yes/no, options A/B; sends to the group and to the Notion Companies database.
- Group chat views (26-27 Oct): Badr-Eddine wants a small fee per meetup paid by companies, pitched as "meetup clé en main, 30 to 40 devs, X DHS", plus a lead-capture site. Mehdi wants an offer beyond the room. Sallah agrees to test but warns against becoming a "boîte d'intérim" and notes hosts already pay catering. Rachid proposes a monthly fee with a promise list, cites societiz.com, and raises the legal question (company vs association).

Source of truth for the partners page, the brochure PDF, and the quotes. Decided on 25 October 2026. Prices in MAD, excluding tax (HT). Quotes state HT and TTC.

## Why partner (the pitch, with evidence)

1. The audience. Each meetup gets 110 to 157 registrations for about 50 seats. The room is engineers, tech leads, and architects from banks, telecoms, consultancies, and software companies in Casablanca. The conference brings 60 of them together for three days.
2. The format. No sales pitch from the stage. Your engineers talk with their peers about what works in production. That is why people come back, and why the audience is worth more than a booth.
3. The track record. Four editions, fourteen meetups, and five partners who sponsored every edition since 2022: Gemography, Keiken, Maltem Africa / Valkima, inwi, McKinsey.

## What the items mean

- Talent list with consent. The Luma registration form asks: "Share my profile with the host company?" After the event, you receive the list of participants who said yes: name, role, company, LinkedIn. Nothing else, nobody who said no.
- Insight note. A two-page written summary of what the engineers said in the marketplace discussions: themes, positions, open questions. Written by the organizers within two weeks.
- Lightning talk slot. 8 minutes, technical, by one of your engineers. The organizers review the title. No product demo.
- Recruitment moment. 5 minutes at the start of the marketplace: who you are, what you build, who you hire. Plus a printed or digital job board at the marketplace wall.
- Host keynote. 15 minutes at the opening: your engineering context, a problem you solved, a question you want the room to discuss.

## FAQ

1. Can we sponsor without attending? Yes. The seats are yours to use or to give to the community tickets.
2. Can we get our logo on stage? Sponsors with a Pack, yes: on-site signage and the opening slide. The sessions themselves have no sponsor branding.
3. Can our CEO give a talk at SoCraTes? No. The open space has no scheduled talks. At a meetup, the host keynote is yours.
4. Do you issue a proper invoice? Yes: MSCA is a registered association with an ICE and an IF. The quote and the invoice state HT and TTC.
5. Is a sponsorship a donation? Some partners book it as sponsoring, some as a donation. We provide the document your accounting needs; tell us which in the call.
6. Can we pay in two instalments? Yes, half at signature and half before the event.
7. What if the conference is cancelled? The amount is refunded or moved to the next edition, your choice.
8. Can we pick the meetup date? Yes, from the 2027 calendar, before 31 January.
9. Can we host two meetups? Yes: one included in the Pack, and a second one at the Host price.
10. Can we have a booth? No. The format is a conversation, not a trade show. The recruitment moment and the talent list do the job better.

## Recommendation on publishing prices

Publish the prices on the site. Reasons: the packs are fixed and not negotiated; partners can self-qualify before the call; it saves the organizers one email per prospect; and transparency matches the community's values. Keep one line of flexibility: "Need a different split? Talk to us."
